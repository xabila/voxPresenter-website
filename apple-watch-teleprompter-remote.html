Apple Watch Stage Remote & Wrist Teleprompter

3-Mode Apple Watch Teleprompter & Wireless Slide Clicker for PowerPoint & Keynote

Leave plastic USB clickers behind. Vox Presenter's Studio Noir Apple Watch app gives you an eyes-free haptic slide remote, an independent Dual-Deck controller for slides + script paragraphs, and a live wrist teleprompter with Digital Crown scrolling.

Designed for Stage Lighting: 3 Swipable Watch Modes

Pure OLED black (#000000) eliminates wrist glare under stage spotlights while Terracotta and Emerald controls remain legible at a glance.

Mode 1 · Slide Remote

Eyes-Free Haptic Clicker

A giant terracotta Next Slide button engineered to be tapped without looking down at your wrist while facing your audience, confirmed by crisp haptic feedback.

Mode 2 · Dual-Deck

Slides + Teleprompter Split

Independent top and bottom decks: advance or rewind your Mac/Windows PC slides on top, and step through or pause your iPhone/iPad teleprompter paragraphs on the bottom.

Mode 3 · Wrist Prompter

Live Script & Digital Crown

Read the currently active paragraph directly on your wrist as your iPhone tracks your voice, or rotate the Digital Crown to scroll smoothly line by line.

Quick Tip: How to Install Vox Presenter on Your Apple Watch

If watchOS automatic app installation is turned off on your iPhone, simply open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll down to Available Apps at the bottom of the My Watch tab, and tap Install next to VoxPresenter.
