FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

How does automatic voice scrolling work?

VoxPresenter listens to your voice in real time directly on your iPhone. As you speak the words of your script, the teleprompter highlights the current paragraph and scrolls automatically. No touching or swiping required.

How does the Apple Watch stage remote work?

The included Apple Watch app features 3 stage-ready modes: Haptic Slide Clicker (to advance slides without looking at your wrist), Dual-Deck View (combined slide and teleprompter paragraph controls), and Wrist Prompter (live active paragraph display with Digital Crown scrolling).

Does the app work offline without internet?

Yes. On devices and languages supported by Apple's on-device speech models, speech recognition runs directly on your iPhone or iPad. Your scripts stay stored locally on your device and work reliably even in airplane mode on stage.

Does the app support mirror mode for teleprompter glass rigs?

Yes. VoxPresenter features both horizontal and vertical mirror modes. Mount your iPhone or iPad under any beam splitter glass rig and look directly into the camera lens with perfectly legible reflected text.

How do I control slides on Mac or Windows PC?

Download the free VoxPresenter Remote companion app for Mac or Windows PC. Devices discover each other automatically over local Wi-Fi. Advance your slides automatically by voice or manually from your iPhone and Apple Watch on PowerPoint, Keynote, or Canva.

What happens if I hesitate or improvise during a speech?

VoxPresenter's voice tracking algorithm is exceptionally forgiving: it accommodates natural pauses, improvisations, and repeats. If you digress, it waits on the current paragraph and picks back up the moment you return to your script.
