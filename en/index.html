Frequently asked questions

How does offline word-by-word voice scrolling work?

VoxPresenter runs neural speech recognition directly on your iPhone or iPad's Apple Silicon chip across 35+ languages. It highlights your spoken position with zero line-wrap shift and pauses automatically whenever you pause or improvise, even in Airplane Mode.

How do emcees handle co-hosts, loud venues, or live running-order changes?

Live Stage Mode keeps upcoming paragraphs visible in a continuous view: tap any upcoming block or open the # Cue Sheet drawer to jump and resync voice immediately. When a co-host speaks, mute voice listening in 1 tap or press the B / . blackout key on any pocket Bluetooth clicker (Logitech, Kensington, Satechi).

How much does VoxPresenter cost compared to PromptSmart or BIGVU?

VoxPresenter is free to download and start. VoxPresenter Pro unlocks everything across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch for $24.99/year (with a 3-day free trial) or $49.99 once as a permanent Lifetime purchase, with no mandatory cloud account.

How do I read PowerPoint (.pptx) speaker notes while advancing slides on Windows or Mac?

Open your .pptx deck in VoxPresenter to extract every slide's notes and chapter titles automatically. Launch the free VoxPresenter Remote utility on your Windows PC or Mac over local Wi-Fi (or iPhone Hotspot) to trigger PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, or Canva by voice or from your Apple Watch.

How does one-handed Apple Watch Double Tap work onstage?

On Apple Watch Series 9+, Series 10+, and Ultra 2+, pinching your index finger and thumb twice triggers your primary slide or cue action one-handed without crossing your other arm across your body.

Does it work with physical 45° beam-splitter mirror rigs?

Yes. Flip horizontally or vertically in one tap on a pure OLED black canvas that eliminates light bleed on Elgato, Neewer, Desview, SmallRig, and broadcast glass rigs.
