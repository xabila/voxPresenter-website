FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything presenters ask before going live

How does VoxPresenter's real-time voice scrolling work?

VoxPresenter runs on-device neural speech recognition directly on your iPhone or iPad. As you speak the words of your script aloud, it highlights your live position with zero layout shift and scrolls smoothly at your exact natural cadence, pausing automatically whenever you pause or improvise.

How do professional emcees handle co-hosts, loud rooms, or last-minute running order changes onstage?

Live Stage Mode (v1.0.33) keeps upcoming paragraphs visible in a continuous script view: tap any upcoming paragraph or open the # Cue Sheet drawer (# Speaker Intro, # Award, # Closing) to jump straight there and resync voice tracking immediately. When a co-host has the mic, pause voice listening in 1 tap or press the B / . blackout button on any pocket Bluetooth presentation clicker (Logitech, Kensington, Satechi).

How can I read PowerPoint (.pptx) speaker notes on my iPhone while advancing slides on a Windows PC or Mac?

Import your .pptx presentation directly into VoxPresenter on iPhone or iPad: every slide's speaker notes and slide titles are extracted automatically with [SLIDE] markers and chapter bookmarks. Open the free VoxPresenter Remote companion on your Windows PC or Mac over local Wi-Fi (or Personal Hotspot) to trigger PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, or Canva slides by voice or from your Apple Watch.

Does VoxPresenter work 100% offline in Airplane Mode on stage?

Yes. Across 35+ languages supported by Apple's on-device speech models, both voice tracking and 4K MP4 video recording run completely offline on your device. Zero audio and zero slide notes are uploaded to any server.

How do the 3 Apple Watch modes and one-handed Double Tap work?

The Studio Noir watchOS app includes an Eyes-Free Haptic Clicker, a Dual-Deck split screen (Slides + Script), and a Digital Crown Wrist Prompter. On Apple Watch Series 9+ and Ultra 2, pinching your index finger and thumb twice (Double Tap) advances your primary cue one-handed so you maintain an open, confident posture onstage.

Why choose VoxPresenter over PromptSmart Pro, BIGVU, Teleprompter.com, or CapCut?

Teleprompter.com and CapCut rely on fixed-speed timers that keep scrolling when you pause. BIGVU is a cloud-heavy social video editor. VoxPresenter combines all of them with a One-Time Lifetime option.
